《怀疑》序言

Doubt: a preface

2025 年 12 月 7 日2025-12-07

This piece is written in Chinese.

《怀疑：一则寓言》最初由曼哈顿剧院俱乐部于2004年11月23日制作/出品。

作者：John Patrick Shanley

译者：Doubtist汉化组

献词 (Dedication)

本剧献给来自众多天主教修会的修女们，她们毕生服务于医院、学校和养老院。尽管她们遭受了诸多非议和嘲笑，但试问我们中又有谁能像她们一样慷慨无私呢？

This play is dedicated to the many orders of Catholic nuns who have devoted their lives to serving others in hospitals, schools and retirement homes. Though they have been much maligned and ridiculed, who among us has been so generous?

序言 (PREFACE)

一部戏剧的基石是什么？是什么在支撑着它？你也可能问，我人生的基石是什么？我是建立在什么之上的？在每个人、每部戏剧的背后，都存在着某种形式的沉默。而任何社群背后，都存在着一些没有明说的东西。

当今的美国社会出现了一种明显的“症状”，你在政治脱口秀、娱乐报道、各种艺术评论和宗教讨论中都可以感受到它的存在——我们现下正生活在一种“法庭文化”中。在过去，我们生活在一种“名人文化”（译者注：指整个社会对所有名流如演员、政治家、主持人等的普遍关注和痴迷）中，但那种文化已经消亡了——如今，我们只关注那些身陷官司的名人。我们当下的文化推崇极端主张、对抗、评判和裁决。友好的讨论让位给了激烈的辩论，交流变成了一场意志的较量，公共场合的对话变得虚伪而令人厌恶。原因何在？或许在这些喧嚣背后我们已经意识到其实我们对一切都一无所知……只是没有人愿意承认罢了。

我来问你，你是否曾在某次争论中死守个人立场，即便这种坚持已让你感到不适？你是否曾为某种你几乎厌倦的生活方式进行辩护？你是否曾为某个你已不再全然相信的信条效力？你是否曾向某个女孩表达爱意的同时，身体却因为言不由衷而感到隐约的恶心？我有过——那是一个奇妙的时刻。对一位剧作家来说，这是一个创意的原点。我发现了创作的空间，那些根植于我人生和这个时代中、未曾言说的事情——我以“怀疑”开篇。

什么是怀疑？我们每个人都像一颗星球，拥有看似永恒的地壳，对自己是谁充满信心。如果你问的话，我们可以轻而易举地描述我们当下的状态。同你一样，我知道我对很多问题都有自己的答案。比方说，你父亲是什么样的人？你相信上帝吗？你最好的朋友是谁？你想要什么？你的答案反映着此刻你星球的地貌，看似永恒，实则不然。

因为在那看似轻松的回答之下，还有另外一个“你”。这个无言的存在无时无刻不在流动；它悄无声息地向上翻涌，直到抗拒的意识别无选择，只能做出让步。

正是怀疑（它最初常常被当作软弱）改变了事物。当一个人感到不再稳定时，当他动摇时，当来之不易的知识在他眼前蒸发时，他正处于成长的边缘。

一个人的外在表现与其内在核心或微妙或激烈的和解，最初通常看起来像是一个错误，就像你走错了路、迷失了一样。但这只是情感对熟悉的渴望。当你那无言的灵魂带着重构一切的力量，冲破思维的僵死惯性时，生命就诞生了。怀疑，无异于一个重新进入“当下”的机会。

我把这部戏的背景设定在 1964 年，那一年，似乎不仅是我，整个世界都在经历着某种巨大的青春期。旧的方式在行为、着装、道德、世界观方面仍占主导地位，但曾经朝气蓬勃的表达却退化成了一个死气沉沉的面具。我当时在纽约布朗克斯一所由慈善修女会管理的天主教教会学校读书。

这群修女身着黑衣，相信地狱的存在，服从于教会中的男性同僚，并承担起教书育人的责任。将我们团结在一起的信仰，早已超出了宗教的范畴。那是一种我们一同称之为“现实”的共同梦想。当时我们没意识到，但我们达成了一种默契，一种社会契约。我们都曾相信同样的事情——我们都相信着。

回首往事，在我看来，似乎在那个时代的天主教学校里，我们是一个不分年纪的整体。我们都是成年人，我们也都是孩子。就像许多动物一样，我们聚集在一起是为了温暖和安全。可结果却是，我们极易受到任何选择捕猎我们的人的伤害。当信任成为主流时，捕食者就可以肆意掠夺。

而他们也确实进行了掠夺。正如逐渐被广泛披露的教会丑闻所揭示的那样，猎手们曾享受着他们的狂欢。那些沉溺于粉饰其表面形象的牧羊人，牺牲了本质的圣善，以换取所谓的美德。

我从未忘记那个时代的教训，也从未充分吸取它们。我仍然渴望一种共同的确定性，一种对安全的假设，一种相信别人比我更清楚什么才是最好的保证。而奇妙人生中那些难以避免的苦难，引导我开始珍视智者的古训：怀疑。

生命中存在着一些令人不安的时期，那时信念已经开始动摇，但伪善尚未占据主导，意识受到干扰，但尚未完全改变。这是人生中最危险、最重要且持续不断的经历。变革始于怀疑的那一刻。 那是一个关键的时刻，在那个时刻我要么重塑我的人性，要么变成一个谎言。

怀疑比信念需要更多的勇气和精力； 因为信念是一个可止步的休憩之所，而怀疑是无限循环的——它是一种充满激情的训练。你可能会带着不确定的感觉走出我的剧场。你可能渴望确定性。请蔑视对确定性的那种渴望，我们必须学会与十足的不确定性共存。 这里没有最终的答案。 那就是我们时代喧嚣之下的沉默。

What's under a play? What holds it up? You might as well ask what's under me? On what am I built? There's something silent under every person and under every play. There is something unsaid under any given society as well.

There's a symptom apparent in America right now. It's evident in political talk shows, in entertainment coverage, in artistic criticism of every kind, in religious discussion. We are living in a courtroom culture. We were living in a celebrity culture, but that's dead. Now we're only interested in celebrities if they're in court. We are living in a culture of extreme advocacy, of confrontation, of judgment, and of verdict. Discussion has given way to debate. Communication has become a contest of wills. Public talking has become obnoxious and insincere. Why? Maybe it's because deep down under the chatter we have come to a place where we know that we don't know . . . anything. But nobody's willing to say that.

Let me ask you. Have you ever held a position in an argument past the point of comfort? Have you ever defended a way of life you were on the verge of exhausting? Have you ever given service to a creed you no longer utterly believed? Have you ever told a girl you loved her and felt the faint nausea of eroding conviction? I have. That's an interesting moment. For a playwright, it's the beginning of an idea. I saw a piece of real estate on which I might build a play, a play that sat on something silent in my life and in my time. I started with a title: Doubt.

What is Doubt? Each of us is like a planet. There's the crust, which seems eternal. We are confident about who we are. If you ask, we can readily describe our current state. I know my answers to so many questions, as do you. What was your father like? Do you believe in God? Who's your best friend? What do you want? Your answers are your current topography, seemingly permanent, but deceptively so.

Because under that face of easy response, there is another You. And this wordless Being moves just as the instant moves; it presses upward without explanation, fluid and wordless, until the resisting consciousness has no choice but to give way.

It is Doubt (so often experienced initially as weakness) that changes things. When a man feels unsteady, when he falters, when hard-won knowledge evaporates before his eyes, he's on the verge of growth.

The subtle or violent reconciliation of the outer person and the inner core often seems at first like a mistake, like you've gone the wrong way and you're lost. But this is just emotion longing for the familiar. Life happens when the tectonic power of your speechless soul breaks through the dead habits of the mind. Doubt is nothing less than an opportunity to reenter the Present.

The play. I've set my story in 1964, when not just me but the whole world seemed to be going through some kind of vast puberty. The old ways were still dominant in behavior, dress, morality, world-view, but what had been organic expression had become a dead mask. I was in a Catholic church school in the Bronx, run by the Sisters of Charity.

These women dressed in black, believed in Hell, obeyed their male counterparts, and educated us. The faith, which held us together, went beyond the precincts of religion. It was a shared dream we agreed to call Reality. We didn't know it, but we had a deal, a social contract. We would all believe the same thing. We would all believe.

Looking back, it seems to me, in those schools at that time, we were an ageless unity. We were all adults, and we were all children. We had, like many animals, flocked together for warmth and safety. As a result, we were terribly vulnerable to anyone who chose to hunt us. When trust is the order of the day, predators are free to plunder.

And plunder they did. As the ever widening Church scandals reveal, the hunters had a field day. And the shepherds, so invested in the surface, sacrificed actual good for perceived virtue.

I have never forgotten the lessons of that era, nor learned them well enough. I still long for a shared certainty, an assumption of safety, the reassurance of believing that others know better than me what's for the best. But I have been led by the bitter necessities of an interesting life to value that age-old practice of the wise: Doubt.

There is an uneasy time when belief has begun to slip but hypocrisy has yet to take hold, when the consciousness is disturbed but not yet altered. It is the most dangerous, important, and ongoing experience of life. The beginning of change is the moment of Doubt. It is that crucial moment when I renew my humanity or become a lie.

Doubt requires more courage than conviction does, and more energy; because conviction is a resting place and doubt is infinite — it is a passionate exercise. You may come out of my play uncertain. You may want to be sure. Look down on that feeling. We've got to learn to live with a full measure of uncertainty. There is no last word. That's the silence under the chatter of our time.
